What the results mean

Distance estimates the total road-network cost represented by your matrix. It is not live navigation distance.

Travel time is estimated as total distance divided by the average speed you enter. Traffic, waiting and road conditions are not included.

Capacity checks total passengers against the selected bus capacity. This demo treats every pickup as a collection stop and does not model drop-offs.

Optimality is guaranteed only by the exact search methods within their supported input limits. Heuristics trade proof of optimality for speed.

How to read this experiment

One route = one permutation. With 7 pickup locations, the number of possible orders is 7! = 5,040. It is 5,040, not 5,070.

Distance calculation. Depot → pickup 1 → … → pickup n → depot. Every leg is looked up in your distance matrix and added to the total.

Optimal route. Brute Force checks all permutations and keeps the smallest total. The catalogue lets you inspect individual candidates rather than only seeing the final answer.

Important. The number shown updates to match the current number of pickup locations. The catalogue is paginated and routes are computed as requested.
